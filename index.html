FAQ

Common Questions

How do I know which level is right for me?

We recommend taking our placement test before registering so we can match you with the right class.

What is the class size?

Most small-group classes are capped at 10 students to preserve quality and interaction.

Can I switch between online and in-person classes?

Yes. Depending on availability, we can usually place you in a format that suits your schedule.

What can you tell me about your instructors and teaching experience?

Our instructors have several years of experience teaching French in both Iran and Canada. They hold advanced certifications and focus on practical teaching to help you speak with confidence.

How long does it take to reach B2 from zero?

With consistent study around 2 hours per day, you should expect at least one year to reach a B2 level.

Can I get a refund if I need to cancel?

Refund policies depend on the class type and the time of cancellation.
